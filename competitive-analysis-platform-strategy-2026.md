## 1. AskDoss.com — Analysis

### What It Is

DOSS (askdoss.com) is an **AI-powered real estate marketplace** built around voice and text interaction — think Siri, but exclusively for real estate. Founded 2016, Houston TX. Backed by Google. Powered by IBM Watson.

Its stated mission: **"centralize, democratize, and humanize big data in real estate."**

### What It Does Well

| Feature | Strength |
|---|---|
| Voice/text AI search | Natural language queries on any property |
| Conversational search | Like Alexa — ask anything, get instant answers |
| Free CRM for agents | Democratizes tools normally behind $$$  |
| Flat fee brokerage | Disrupts traditional 3% commission model |
| Data depth | MLS-connected, property documents, predictive analytics |
| "For Agent, By Agent" positioning | Strong trust signal for professionals |

### What It Lacks / Gaps

| Gap | Implication |
|---|---|
| **No standalone calculators** | Users must know to ask — no discovery flow |
| **Heavy reliance on AI chat** | Doesn't serve users who want self-directed tools |
| **Unclear calculator UX** | No mortgage, affordability, ROI, rent-vs-buy tools visible |
| **Currently "Coming Soon" on main site** | Lost traffic, missed first impressions |
| **U.S.-centric** | No international market coverage |
| **No content/SEO play visible** | Invisible in AEO/GEO landscape |
| **No sustainability/insurance calculators** | Misses 2026's biggest cost drivers |

### Verdict on AskDoss

DOSS is technologically forward — an AI-first real estate advisor at a time when AI is table stakes. But it is **a technology product looking for a content and calculator strategy**, not a destination where users naturally discover tools. The gap between its AI capability and its discoverable utility is wide.

## 4. SEO + AEO + GEO Strategy for Real Estate 2026

### The New Search Paradigm

| Channel | What It Does | Share of Traffic |
|---|---|---|
| **SEO** | Ranks in traditional Google results | Still 70–80% of organic traffic |
| **AEO** | Appears in AI-generated featured answers, snippets | Fast-growing; ~15–20% of research queries |
| **GEO** | Cited by ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Copilot | ChatGPT alone = 95% of AI referral traffic |

**Key stat: pages with both strong SEO + AEO optimization receive 2.3× more total search visibility.**

### SEO Strategy

**A. Technical Foundation**
- Core Web Vitals: LCP < 2.5s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.1
- Mobile-first indexing (70%+ real estate searches are mobile)
- JSON-LD schema for: `RealEstateListing`, `FAQPage`, `HowTo`, `LocalBusiness`, `Product` (calculators)
- Faceted navigation handling (canonical tags, noindex for thin pages)
- Internal linking between calculators ↔ neighborhood guides ↔ blog content

**B. Content Architecture**
```
Tier 1: City/Region landing pages (high volume)
  └─ "Apartments for sale in Barcelona"
Tier 2: Neighborhood landing pages (medium volume, high intent)
  └─ "Buy apartment in Eixample Barcelona"
Tier 3: Guide/Intent content (long-tail, AEO/GEO fuel)
  └─ "Is it better to buy or rent in Madrid in 2026?"
  └─ "How much does it cost to buy a flat in Valencia?"
Tier 4: Calculator pages (tool + content hybrid)
  └─ "Mortgage calculator Spain 2026"
  └─ "Rent vs buy calculator Madrid"
```

**C. Hyperlocal SEO (Beating Idealista in Niches)**
- Create the deepest neighborhood guides on the internet
- Include: schools, walkability, public transport, average prices, rental yields, insurance costs
- Update quarterly with real market data
- Use original data to force AI citation (GEO signal)

### AEO Strategy

**Answer-first content** — structure every guide to answer the most common user questions directly and concisely at the top.

Target AEO for these high-value query patterns:
```
"What is the average rent in [city]?"
"How much deposit do I need to buy a house in Spain?"
"Is [neighborhood] a good investment?"
"What are the hidden costs of buying property in Spain?"
"How do I calculate rental yield?"
```

**AEO Technical Tactics**
- Every calculator page has an FAQ section with 5–10 Q&As in FAQ schema
- All guides structured with: Direct Answer (1 sentence) → Context (2–3 sentences) → Full Explanation
- Use `speakable` schema for voice assistant optimization
- Target "People Also Ask" boxes with short, definitive answers
- Build a dedicated "Ask a Question" tool that generates SEO content from user queries

### GEO Strategy (Be Cited by AI)

**The core principle: if you have the data, AI must cite you.**

Tactics:
1. **Publish original market data quarterly** — average prices by neighborhood, rental yields, days on market. This data gets cited because no one else has it.
2. **Create "definitive guide" content** — comprehensive, well-structured, regularly updated. LLMs prioritize these.
3. **Named entity optimization** — become the authoritative entity for "[city] real estate" in knowledge graphs
4. **Press and backlinks** — AI models are trained on high-authority sources; earned media matters
5. **Wikipedia / Wikidata** — ensure your brand, data, and neighborhoods are properly represented
6. **Structured data everywhere** — JSON-LD helps AI parse your content accurately

**GEO Citation Targets:** ChatGPT (95% of AI traffic), Perplexity (~2%), Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot

### 5.2 Content Strategy — Depth Beats Volume

**Each neighborhood gets a complete profile:**
- Average sale price (historical chart, 5 years)
- Average rental yield
- Price per m² vs. city average
- School ratings
- Walkability / transport score
- Days on market (liquidity indicator)
- Insurance risk score
- "Is it better to buy or rent here in 2026?" — answered with data

This content becomes the AEO/GEO fuel. Idealista publishes market data. You publish **decisions** backed by data.

### 5.4 Trust Infrastructure — Beating Fotocasa's Sello

Fotocasa's quality certification is shallow (700 agencies, annual renewal). Build something deeper:

**Verified Agent System:**
- Transaction history verification (not just self-reported)
- Response time tracking (publicly displayed)
- Client review verification (tied to completed transactions)
- Specialization badges (first-time buyers, investors, luxury, international)
- Code of conduct with enforcement (not just pledge)

**Transparent Listing Data:**
- Price history (every price change, timestamped)
- Days on market (hidden by many portals — show it)
- "Fair Price" indicator: is this listing above/below market?
- Energy efficiency rating prominently displayed
- Insurance risk score by postcode
